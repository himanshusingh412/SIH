# Future Product Roadmap — ContentSpine AI

## Phase 1: SIH 2026 Hackathon Prototype (Completed ✅)
* Single Source of Truth Content Spine Architecture.
* Fact Lock Layer & 8-category Consistency Validator.
* Multi-channel generation across 7 formats.
* 4-Tier Source Lineage Inspector (`[Why was this generated?]`).
* Comprehensive Export Modal & 100% Offline Demo Mode.

---

## Phase 2: Post-Hackathon Enterprise Enhancement (Q4 2026)
* **Remotion Worker Integration**: Render production-ready MP4 videos directly from Video Package storyboards.
* **OCR Language Expansion**: Add support for 22 official Indian regional languages (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, etc.) using Tesseract/EasyOCR adapters.
* **Vector Semantic Search**: Integrate Qdrant / Pinecone vector storage for multi-document RAG retrieval across 1,000+ page archives.

---

## Phase 3: Government Deployment (Q1 2027)
* **NIC Cloud & On-Premise Deployment**: Containerized Kubernetes Helm deployment for National Informatics Centre (NIC) data centers.
* **OAuth2 / GovConnect SSO**: Role-based access control for ministry officials, operators, and reviewers.
* **Blockchain Hash Immutability**: Store Content Spine fact lock hashes on National Blockchain Infrastructure for auditability.
